The core idea

It feels like tennis because coverage matters.

Every brick is defensive coverage. Every missing brick is open court. RALLY turns brick-breaker contact into a tennis decision: finish now, or keep the exchange alive for a bigger payoff.

01

Break coverage

The opponent's grid shifts and repairs itself. You are not clearing a wall; you are pulling a defender out of position.

02

Build Rally Depth

Each net crossing raises the counter. At depth six and beyond, one clean gap can feel like a championship point.

03

Finish the gap

A safe finish banks the point. A greedy rally can multiply it. That tension is the whole game.

Tennis texture

Court, net, surface, opponent, shot.

RALLY does not use tennis scoring language. It earns the sport through the court: hard-court speed, clay patience, grass deflections, asphalt acceleration, a visible net, and shots that match one-handed gestures.

Hard Clay Grass Asphalt

Shot language

One hand, real tennis intent.

RALLY keeps shots readable: no swipe is flat, upward pressure adds topspin, downward pressure slices, and advanced finishes change when the gap is worth the risk.

Flat

Direct, reliable, honest contact.

Topspin

Heavy arc that pushes through coverage.

Slice

Low, skidding control into awkward lanes.

Lob

High clearance when the grid is too dense.

Drop

Soft touch for a sudden short finish.

Smash

A depth-gated exclamation point.

Complete modes
