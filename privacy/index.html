RALLY Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Translations are provided for convenience. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version controls.

Data stored on your device

RALLY stores gameplay progress, tutorial status, settings, purchase entitlement state, mode records, and accessibility preferences locally on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. RALLY receives entitlement status so the game can unlock purchased content and restore purchases.

Optional rewarded ads

If rewarded ads are enabled, RALLY uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform for consent and ad delivery. Ads are optional and user-initiated. In regions where consent is required, ads are not requested until the consent flow allows ad requests.
